What I'm building

Infivu is now in private beta for small businesses. Here's the idea in one picture: one website, a different first impression for each visitor.

Private beta

Infivu

Infivu spends its first couple of weeks just learning who visits a site. Then it creates personalized versions of pages for different visitor groups, tests every one against the original, and keeps only what brings in more customers. Owners stay in charge and can give feedback in plain English.
